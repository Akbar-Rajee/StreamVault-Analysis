# Findings

## Finding 1: Franchise Films Outperform Standalone Films

The franchise recommendation remained stable across:

- All-years analysis
- 2000-onward analysis
- Median-based analysis

Franchise films generated higher revenue, profit, and ROI than standalone films across all tested views.

---

## Finding 2: Recommendation Does Not Depend on Older Films

The top franchise candidates remained largely unchanged after restricting the analysis to films released from 2000 onward.

This reduces the risk associated with historical reporting differences.

---

## Finding 3: The Avengers Collection Is the Strongest Franchise Candidate

The Avengers Collection achieved the highest average profit among qualifying franchises.

Average profit was the single most influential metric supporting its recommendation.

---

## Finding 4: Harry Potter Provides Strong Evidence Stability

The Harry Potter Collection contained eight analyzed films, providing the largest evidence base among the leading franchise candidates.

This increases confidence that observed performance is not driven by a small number of titles.

---

## Finding 5: Several Talent Signals Appear Consistently

Strong commercial performance repeatedly appeared alongside:

Directors:

- Peter Jackson
- David Yates
- Christopher Nolan

Lead Actors:

- Robert Downey Jr.
- Daniel Radcliffe
- Tom Hanks

These individuals appeared across multiple commercially successful titles.

---

## Finding 6: Data Quality Issues Were Present

The candidate list contained:

- Duplicate IDs
- Missing IDs
- Invalid IDs
- An IMDb ID mixed into TMDb identifiers
- Unretrievable TMDb IDs

These issues required remediation before analysis.
