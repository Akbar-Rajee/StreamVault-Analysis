# ADR-003: Franchise Shortlist Eligibility Threshold

## Status

Accepted

## Problem

Several collections contained only one analysed film.

Their rankings were heavily influenced by a single movie rather than franchise-level performance.

## Impact

The shortlist could become dominated by outliers rather than sustained franchise success.

## Decision

Only collections with at least three analysed titles were considered eligible for the franchise shortlist.

## Alternative Considered

### Include All Collections

Allow every collection to compete regardless of available evidence.

### Why Someone Might Choose It

It maximises the number of franchise opportunities considered.

### Why Rejected

Single-title collections provide weak evidence and unstable rankings.

## Trade-Off

The shortlist becomes more reliable but may exclude smaller opportunities.

## Cost if Wrong

Potentially valuable franchises with limited representation may be excluded.

## Evidence

This threshold removed collections that ranked highly based on a single successful film and shifted focus toward repeatable franchise performance.
