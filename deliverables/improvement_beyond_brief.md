# Improvement Beyond the Brief

## Automated Reconciliation and Data Quality Reporting

### Overview

An automated reconciliation and data quality report was added to the workflow.

Each execution produces a summary showing:

- Candidate rows received
- Duplicate IDs detected
- Invalid IDs detected
- Missing IDs detected
- Valid IDs submitted for retrieval
- Successful retrievals
- Failed retrievals
- Analysis coverage rate

---

## Value to Kwame

The licensing recommendation depends on complete and trustworthy data.

Without reconciliation reporting, titles can be silently lost during acquisition or transformation, potentially changing recommendations without anyone noticing.

The report provides immediate visibility into:

- How much of the candidate list was successfully analyzed
- Whether any films were excluded
- The size of the uncertainty introduced by missing data

This improves confidence during board-level decision making.

---

## Value to Adjoa

The brief states that Adjoa will rerun the analysis quarterly and that future candidate lists may approach 500 titles.

Manually validating hundreds of records every quarter is inefficient and error-prone.

The automated report allows Adjoa to verify data quality immediately after each run without manually inspecting raw files.

Benefits include:

- Faster reruns
- Reduced risk of unnoticed data loss
- Easier troubleshooting
- Better scalability for larger candidate lists

---

## Measurable Benefit

The improvement converts data validation from a manual review activity into an automated quality-control process.

Rather than discovering missing or invalid records during analysis or meetings, issues are identified immediately after acquisition, reducing operational risk and improving the reliability of future recommendations.
